## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Machine Learning Research Engineer** and **Full-Stack AI Developer** holding a Bachelor of Science in **Computer Science & Engineering (B.Sc. in CSE)** from **Varendra University (VU)** in Rajshahi, Bangladesh. My work connects theoretical algorithmic innovation with scalable, low-latency production engineering.

- 🔬 **Undergraduate Research & Scholarly Impact:** Active undergraduate researcher at Varendra University with **9+ peer-reviewed publications** indexed across **IEEE Xplore** and **Springer Nature** (**13+ citations** on Google Scholar). My research advances applied deep learning, multimodal medical image classification, document intelligence, Indic handwritten OCR, and real-time aquatic epidemiology.
- 🤖 **OncoVision AI & Clinical Diagnostic Copilot:** Architected an enterprise-level histopathology platform for automated Lung and Colon Cancer classification that integrates a **grounded clinical LLM chatbot** powered by Google Gemini and domain-specific RAG. The system explains inference probabilities, drafts natural-language clinical rationales, and provides verified literature citations under strict medical safety guardrails.
- 🐟 **Aquatic Epidemiology & Environmental AI:** Engineered a scalable deep learning computer vision framework for real-time fish disease detection, early outbreak prevention, and climate resilience in commercial aquaculture (published at IEEE ICCIT 2025).
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & OCR Specialization:** Extensive track record in designing custom CNN backbones, Transfer Learning pipelines, and polyarchitectural ensembles for optical character recognition, reaching benchmark-shattering accuracies up to **99.97%** on handwritten digit datasets (NumtaDB).
- ⚡ **Full-Stack Production Engineering:** Deploying research models into production web applications using **FastAPI**, **React 19 / 18**, and the **MERN Stack**. Experienced in edge optimization (88% TFLite model quantization, decoupled Hugging Face Hub runtime loading, TanStack Query 0ms prefetching, and Vercel edge reverse proxies).
- 🏆 **Algorithmic Foundations:** Competitive programmer and **ICPC Asia Dhaka Regional Contestant (2023)**. Proficient in designing time- and space-optimal algorithms in C++ and Python to accelerate mission-critical inference pipelines.

### 🎯 Core Research Pillars

1. **Document Intelligence & Optical Character Recognition (OCR):**
   - Pioneering primary dataset collection, deskewing, noise reduction, and projection profile segmentation for Indic and Bangla handwritten scripts.
   - Engineering diversified deep convolutional ensemble networks (VGG19, InceptionV3, DenseNet) that overcome compound script ambiguity and low-fidelity scans, achieving up to **99.97%** accuracy on the NumtaDB benchmark.
2. **Medical Digital Histopathology & Neuro-Oncology:**
   - Designing multimodal feature fusion architectures combining complementary spatial representations from **EfficientNetV2** and **ResNet50**, achieving **100% precision and accuracy** in stratifying complex Lung and Colon Cancer biopsy samples.
   - Multi-class Brain Tumor MRI scan classification with transfer learning yielding **99.31% accuracy** and **99.35% F1-score**.
3. **Aquatic Epidemiology & Aquaculture AI:**
   - Designing scalable deep learning diagnostic frameworks for aquatic pathogen and disease detection, preventing rapid commercial aquaculture outbreaks and bolstering climate-environmental resilience.
4. **Federated & Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning:**
   - Algorithmic research on decentralized, federated training across clinical institutions to safeguard patient privacy while addressing statistical non-IID data divergence.
5. **Attention Transformers & Agro-Economics:**
   - Applying long-sequence Informer attention transformers to global food commodity price forecasting (World Food Programme datasets) and agroinformatics weather anomaly detection.
